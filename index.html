About the Conference

The Conference on Nonlinear Partial Differential Equations and Applications 2027 will be held at the University of New England in Armidale, Australia. The conference will bring together researchers working in nonlinear partial differential equations and related areas.

The aim of the conference is to provide a friendly and stimulating environment in which researchers can present recent developments, exchange ideas, explore new research directions, and establish collaborations. The program will include invited lectures and contributed talks.

Researchers at all career stages, including postgraduate students and early-career researchers, are warmly invited to participate.

Conference Topics

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Nonlinear partial differential equations;
  • Reaction–diffusion equations and systems;
  • Free boundary problems;
  • Propagation phenomena and travelling waves;
  • Nonlocal and fractional diffusion equations;
  • Elliptic and parabolic equations;
  • Mathematical biology and population dynamics;
  • Dynamical systems arising from partial differential equations;
  • Related analytical and numerical methods.
