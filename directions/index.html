Getting to Armidale

Armidale is located in the New England region of northern New South Wales. Participants may travel to Armidale by air, car, coach, or train.

By Air

Armidale Airport is the nearest airport to the University of New England. Participants should check current airline schedules when arranging their travel, as flight availability may vary.

The airport is a short drive from both the Armidale city centre and the University of New England. Taxis and other local transport options may be available at the airport.

By Car

Armidale can be reached by road from Sydney, Brisbane, Newcastle, Tamworth, and other regional centres. Participants travelling by car are advised to allow sufficient time and check road conditions before departure.

When using a navigation system, enter:

University of New England, Armidale NSW 2351

By Train or Coach

Regional train and coach services may operate to Armidale. Participants should consult the relevant transport provider for current timetables, connections, and booking information.

The Armidale railway station is located near the city centre. Local transport will be required to travel from the station to the University of New England.

Local Transport

The University of New England is located several kilometres from the Armidale city centre. Participants may use taxis, rideshare services, local buses, private vehicles, or arranged conference transport where available.

Details of any conference shuttle or organised transport will be announced later.

Parking

Parking is available in several areas of the University of New England campus. Some parking areas may require a permit or may have time restrictions.

Participants travelling by car should check the final conference information before arrival. Details of the recommended parking area will be provided after the conference venue has been confirmed.

Campus Map

A campus map showing the conference building, registration desk, parking areas, catering locations, and other important facilities will be added to this page closer to the conference.

Accommodation

Participants are generally responsible for arranging and paying for their own accommodation. Accommodation options are available both on or near the University campus and in the Armidale city centre.

A list of suggested accommodation options will be provided after the conference dates and venue have been finalised.

Participants should book accommodation early, particularly if the conference coincides with other major events in Armidale.

Weather and Clothing

Armidale is located at a relatively high elevation and can be cooler than many other Australian cities. Temperatures may change considerably between daytime and evening.

Participants are advised to check the weather forecast shortly before travelling and bring suitable warm clothing, especially if the conference is held during the cooler months.

Accessibility and Special Requirements

Participants who require accessible parking, mobility assistance, dietary arrangements, or other support are encouraged to contact the organising committee before the conference.

Travel Enquiries

For questions about the venue, transport, accommodation, or local arrangements, please contact the organising committee.
